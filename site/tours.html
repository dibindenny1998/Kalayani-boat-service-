Price for your group.

Extra guests pay ₹100 per hour on most three-hour tours, and ₹150 per hour on the Half Day Tour, Full Day Tour and Lunch Cruise.

Where do we board?

All tours start and end at Rajiv Boat Jetty, Alappuzha.

How is the price calculated?

Each price is for two guests. Every extra guest adds ₹100 per hour on most three-hour tours, and ₹150 per hour on the Half Day Tour, Full Day Tour and Lunch Cruise.

Can we choose our own time?

Yes. The Regular Cruise runs for any three hours you choose, at ₹1,800 for two.

How long is a ride?

At least 3 hours. Every ride starts and ends at Rajiv Boat Jetty.

How do we book?

Send us your tour, date and number of guests on WhatsApp, by phone or with the booking form. We confirm availability.
