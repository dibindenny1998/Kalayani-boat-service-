Ask the boatman

Beforeyou board

What is included in the fare?

Kerala breakfast, Tea or coffee, and the boat for 4 hours with our crew. The fare of ₹2,300 covers two guests.

What if we are more than two?

Each extra guest adds ₹150 for every hour, so ₹600 per person on this 4-hour ride.

What time should we reach the jetty?

Boarding at 8:00 am, back at 12:00 pm. Please be at Rajiv Boat Jetty ten minutes early.

What should we bring?

Sunglasses, a hat and sunscreen for daytime rides, and a light layer for the early morning.
