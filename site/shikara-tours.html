How pricing works

One price for two, then per hour.

Each tour has a price for two guests. Every extra guest adds a fixed amount for each hour: ₹100 per hour on the three-hour cruises and ₹150 per hour on the half-day and full-day tours.

Example: the Sunrise Cruise for four guests is ₹1,500 + 2 × ₹100 × 3 hrs = ₹2,100.
