The boat

On deckof the Kalyani

A shikara is the small covered boat of Kerala's backwater: low in the water, shaded, built for sitting back and watching the banks go by.

Look around

Tap themarkers

  • Coir canopyA woven coconut-fibre roof keeps the deck shaded through the day.
  • Life buoysRings hang under the canopy, within reach of every seat.
  • Cane chairsCovered cane chairs for groups, facing the water on both sides.
  • Turned teak pillarsCarved wooden pillars hold up the canopy along the length of the boat.
  • Open waterLow rails, so the backwater is at eye level from your seat.
