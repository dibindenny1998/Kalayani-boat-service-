Shikara: the small boat of the backwaters

A shikara sits low on the water and slips into village canals the big houseboats can't reach. You lie back on the deck and Alleppey drifts past.

Kalyani Boat Service runs shikara cruises and kayaking from Rajiv Boat Jetty in Alappuzha. Our shikaras have a cushioned front deck, cane chairs and a woven coir canopy, and every tour passes the Snake Boat Race track before crossing Vembanad Lake.

“Pick your hour. The backwater looks different at every one.”
