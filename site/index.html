Price for your group.

Each price is for two guests. Extra guests pay per hour: ₹100 on three-hour tours, ₹150 on the Half Day and Full Day.

Where do we board?

All tours start and end at Rajiv Boat Jetty, Alappuzha.

How is the price calculated?

Each price is for two guests. Every extra guest adds ₹100 per hour on the three-hour tours and ₹150 per hour on the Half Day and Full Day tours.

Can we choose our own time?

Yes. The Regular Cruise runs for any three hours you choose, at ₹1,500 for two.

How do we book?

Send us your tour, date and number of guests on WhatsApp, by phone or with the booking form. We confirm availability.
