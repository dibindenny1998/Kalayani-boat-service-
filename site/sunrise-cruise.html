Questions

Before you go.

Where do we board the shikara?

All tours start and end at Rajiv Boat Jetty, Alappuzha. We share the exact meeting point on WhatsApp when we confirm your booking.

How is the price worked out?

₹1,500 covers two guests. Each extra guest adds ₹100 for each hour of the tour, so ₹300 per extra guest on this 3-hour tour.

What time does the tour run?

Starts at 6:00 am and returns at 9:00 am.

How do I book?

Send us your date and number of guests on WhatsApp or with the form on this page. We reply to confirm availability and the booking details.
